## Overview

Nopload is a free, ad-free image suite where every byte is processed in your browser. It removes
backgrounds, compresses, converts (JPG, PNG, WebP, AVIF, JXL), resizes, turns HEIC into JPG and
strips EXIF data. Dropping files anywhere on the home page asks what to do with them and hands them
to the chosen tool. It works offline after the first visit and has light and dark themes.

## Architecture

- Static, prerendered Astro pages with Preact islands and Tailwind CSS.
- Background removal runs MODNet and U2-Net-P through onnxruntime-web, on WebGPU when available and
  on the CPU with WASM otherwise.
- jSquash codecs (MozJPEG, OxiPNG, WebP, AVIF, JXL) and libheif-js for HEIC run in Comlink Web
  Workers. exifr reads metadata and fflate builds ZIP downloads.
- A Workbox service worker makes it an installable, offline-capable PWA.

## Hard problems

- **Prove nothing is uploaded, rather than ask for trust.** The CSP in `public/_headers` sets
  `connect-src 'self'`, the header badge lists every request, and `tests/e2e/privacy.spec.ts` fails
  on any request that leaves the site's origin.
- **Full-resolution background removal with no server.** MODNet and U2-Net-P, pinned in
  `models.lock.json`, run through onnxruntime-web on WebGPU, or on the CPU with WASM.
- **Hand files between tools without leaving copies.** `tests/e2e/handoff-privacy.spec.ts` checks
  that handed-off files are deleted from IndexedDB on pickup.

## Trade-offs

- No server means nothing is uploaded, but the model and codecs ship to the browser and are cached
  for offline use.
- WebGPU is used when present, with the slower WASM path as the fallback.

## Verification

- Every page is served with `Content-Security-Policy: ... connect-src 'self'` (see
  `public/_headers`), so the page cannot send images to another origin.
- A "0 uploads" badge lists every network request the page has made.
- The e2e suite fails if any request leaves the site's own origin on any page
  (`tests/e2e/privacy.spec.ts`), during model load and inference
  (`tests/e2e/remove-background.spec.ts`), or offline (`tests/e2e/pwa.spec.ts`).
- Files handed between tools are checked to be deleted from IndexedDB on pickup.

## What it does for you

Drop a photo and Nopload removes its background, shrinks it, converts it, resizes it, turns an
iPhone HEIC photo into a JPG or strips its location data. All of it happens inside your browser:
your image never leaves your device, there is no signup and there are no credits or limits. After
the first visit it even works offline.

## Who it helps

Anyone who needs to edit a photo and would rather not hand it to an upload site, an account or a
credit system.

## Design notes

- Drop files anywhere on the home page and Nopload asks what to do with them, then hands them to
  the chosen tool.
- Every result offers a next step, for example "Next: compress it".
- A "0 uploads" badge in the header opens a panel listing every request the page has made.
- Light and dark themes, keyboard accessible, and audited on a 360 px phone for touch targets and
  focus.
