## Overview

SyncPlay is a Windows desktop music player with a synchronized, word-level lyric overlay, theme
customization and offline forced-alignment tools. It plays MP3, WAV, OGG and FLAC with repeat modes,
shuffle, folder loading and system tray controls. No audio or lyrics are bundled with the
repository: you bring your own files.

## Architecture

- A QMediaPlayer audio engine, with a 30 Hz playback clock driving the overlay.
- Lyrics use `.wlrc`, a custom JSON format of word-level timestamps, stored in MP3 ID3 tags
  (`TXXX:WLRC`) with mutagen.
- The overlay is a transparent, always-on-top window with click-and-hold drag and a resize grip
  (Win32), and QPainter renders the word-level highlighting.
- An alignment tool runs stable-ts (Whisper) forced alignment offline, with optional CUDA
  acceleration, and embeds the result into the MP3 automatically.
- Themes (6 built-in presets plus custom themes) and a subtitle viewer for `.wlrc` files.

## Hard problems

- **Highlight the exact word being sung.** `models/wlrc.py` parses word-level timestamps and searches
  them by binary search; `audio/clock.py` ticks at 30 Hz and
  `overlay/lyric_renderer.py` draws each word with QPainter.
- **Get word timings without typing them.** `alignment/aligner.py` runs stable-ts (Whisper) forced
  alignment offline, with optional CUDA, and `models/wlrc_embed.py` writes the result into the MP3
  as `TXXX:WLRC` with mutagen.
- **Stay on top without getting in the way.** The overlay is click-through by default; holding for
  300 ms enters drag mode, and a grip in the corner resizes it.

## Trade-offs

- It targets Windows 10 and 11 only, in exchange for Win32 overlay behaviour such as click-through.
- The audio visualizer animates decorative bars and is not driven by the audio spectrum yet.

## Verification

- 118 unit tests, run with `pytest music_player/tests/ -v`.
- The overlay is click-through by default, and holding for 300ms enters drag mode.

## What it does for you

Play your own music and the lyrics float over your desktop, lighting up word by word as they are
sung. If a song has no timed lyrics, SyncPlay can line the words up with the audio for you,
offline, and save them inside the MP3.

## Who it helps

Windows listeners who want to sing along to their own music, word by word.

## Design notes

- The overlay sizes itself to the longest line and fades out after 5 seconds of pause.
- 6 built-in themes, from Spotify Green to Cyberpunk, plus custom themes with a live preview on the
  overlay.
- Keyboard shortcuts for play, open file, the overlay and settings, and controls in the system
  tray.
- SyncPlay has no screenshot yet; its card shows a labelled illustration of the overlay.
